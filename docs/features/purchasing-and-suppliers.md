# Purchasing and Suppliers

## Purpose

This module supports the purchasing side of the business, allowing companies to manage suppliers and purchase orders.

## Main Resources

### Suppliers
- store supplier corporate and contact information
- support identification and transaction history

### Purchase Orders
- represent purchase requests or orders sent to suppliers
- contain totals, expected delivery date, and payment status

### Purchase Order Items
- store products, quantities, and unit costs for each purchase order

## Expected Workflows

- register suppliers
- create purchase orders for incoming inventory
- manage expected delivery dates and payment status
- track ordered quantities and costs

## Notes

This module complements the sales flow and is essential for stock replenishment and cost control.
