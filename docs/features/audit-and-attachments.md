# Audit and Attachments

## Purpose

This module ensures traceability, compliance, and document support across the platform.

## Main Resources

### Audit Logs
- record important actions and changes in the system
- support operational traceability
- make it easier to understand who changed what and when

### Attachments
- store files linked to business entities
- support invoices, contracts, product images, and proofs of activity

## Expected Workflows

- log user actions for critical entities
- attach supporting documents to operations
- keep historical evidence for audits and reviews

## Notes

Auditability is a central requirement for a business system, especially for inventory, finance, and fiscal operations.
