# Fiscal and Document Management

## Purpose

This module supports fiscal document handling, numbering, and document flow. It is designed to support Brazilian-style fiscal operations and future integrations.

## Main Resources

### Tax Rules
- define tax rules for products and transactions
- store tax type and rate

### Digital Certificates
- manage digital certificate metadata used for fiscal operations
- support certificate lifecycle and status control

### Document Series
- manage document numbering series per document type
- support current number control

### Numbering Sequences
- support general sequence management for document numbering

### Invoices
- represent fiscal or accounting invoices
- associate invoices to orders, customers, tax rules, and certificates

### Invoice Items
- store invoice lines and values per product

## Expected Workflows

- configure fiscal rules and certificates
- generate or register invoices
- maintain document numbering continuity
- link invoices with orders and tax information

## Notes

This module is aligned with the project's aim to support fiscal document issuance and future integration with external fiscal services.
