# Finance and Payments

## Purpose

This module addresses money movement, cash management, and payment tracking for the business.

## Main Resources

### Payment Methods
- define the available payment methods for the company
- support consistent payment processing

### Financial Accounts
- represent bank accounts or cash accounts
- track opening and current balances

### Financial Transactions
- record movements across financial accounts
- support links to orders, invoices, or other business operations

### Price Rules
- define pricing strategies such as discounts, promotions, or special pricing
- support configurable commercial rules

## Expected Workflows

- configure payment methods
- create financial accounts
- record transactions and balances
- apply price rules to products or business contexts

## Notes

The finance layer is intentionally practical and scalable, supporting both basic accounting control and future expansion into accounting workflows.
