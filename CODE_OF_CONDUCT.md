# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Our expectations

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive in discussions
- assume good intent and communicate clearly
- avoid harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- focus on the project and the problem being solved

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- insults, threats, or discriminatory language
- harassment, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact
- publishing private information without consent
- disruptive behavior that undermines collaboration

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, or pull requests that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from the project community.

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this policy, please contact the maintainers through the project’s preferred communication channel.
